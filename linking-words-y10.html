The five joints

4 min

Each linking word has a job. It also has grammar: the tag next to each word shows what has to come after it.

Start,
Starts a sentence, with a comma after it.
However, …
+ clause
Joins two full clauses.
although it rains, …
+ noun
Needs a noun or -ing form after it.
despite the rain, …
mid
Sits inside the sentence.
they also …

✓Although school starts early, most students arrive on time.

✓Despite the early start, most students arrive on time.

✗Despite school starts early, most students arrive on time.

Handle with care

obviously, clearly, undoubtedly, of course — these don't link two ideas. They tell the reader that everyone already agrees, so a sceptical reader starts looking for the weak spot. Use them only when the point really is beyond argument.

apparently means anscheinend, not offensichtlich.

Build a paragraph

10 min

Choose an opinion.

Write 5–6 sentences arguing for it. Use at least three joints. Two of them must be Concede (admit one point for the other side, then answer it) and Conclude.
